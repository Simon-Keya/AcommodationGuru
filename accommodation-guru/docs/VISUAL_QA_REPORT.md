# Visual QA Report

## Visual direction implemented

- Warm ivory base with sand-toned editorial section, muted gold accents, deep ocean teal and charcoal.
- Large serif display typography paired with a restrained sans-serif body face.
- Full-bleed lifestyle hero with dark readable overlay and clear primary/secondary actions.
- The three How It Works steps each include a relevant image, matching all three image markers in the client content document.
- Featured Getaways uses three editorial image panels, within the client-requested range of two to three images.
- The three How It Works images and final CTA now use clean photographic crops from the two earlier generated concepts. The generated page mockups themselves are not used as assets; all embedded headlines, navigation, forms, buttons and labels were excluded from the crops.
- `watamu.jpg` is the hero background and `hero.jpg` is used in the final CTA. Both are clean, text-free photographs; live headings and actions remain HTML content layered over the images. The three Featured Getaways panels now use the user-saved Favorites photographs: a coastal villa, a stone cottage and a garden retreat. Earlier bundled and generated images remain in the project.
- Generous whitespace, fine divider lines, image-led feature panels and restrained hover transitions.
- Mobile layout changes to stacked cards, full-width actions and single-column enquiry fields.

## Asset review

The hero and CTA photographs, three user-saved Favorites photographs, and earlier generated photographic crops were visually inspected and mapped to the homepage positions. The previously bundled `diani.jpg`, `karen.jpg`, `naivasha.jpg` and `featured-section.jpg` remain available in the assets folder. The images are useful hospitality/lifestyle imagery, but the content document does not establish that they are actual client-owned or contracted listings. They are therefore labelled/described as inspiration imagery rather than named inventory.

## Responsive and accessibility provisions

- Breakpoints at 900px, 760px and 420px.
- Responsive typography and image layouts.
- Semantic sections, labelled form controls, dialog titles, close controls and keyboard-focus styles.
- Reduced-motion preference respected.
- Form labels and required fields included; checkout date has a minimum tied to arrival date.
