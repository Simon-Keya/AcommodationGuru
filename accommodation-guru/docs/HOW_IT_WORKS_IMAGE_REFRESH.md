# How It Works image refresh

Replaced the three generic images in the homepage “Your Perfect Stay, Made Simple” steps with original photorealistic generated images.

- **Tell Us What You’re Looking For:** guest planning a getaway on a laptop from a tropical terrace.
- **We Find Your Perfect Match:** bright, welcoming tropical villa bedroom representing the accommodation match.

The existing step titles, copy, layout, CTA, and design system were preserved. The images were resized and optimized as progressive JPEGs and retain the existing filenames, so no component or configuration changes were needed.

These are AI-generated illustrative hospitality scenes, not photographs of specific listed properties.
